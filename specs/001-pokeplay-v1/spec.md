# PokePlay V1 Website Spec

## Objective
Launch a small SEO-focused website for PokePlay Studios, with PokePlay Live promoting the Whatnot stream and PokePlay Store pointing buyers to current listings while preparing for future storefront expansion.

## Requirements
- Use `pokeplaystudios.com` as the canonical site domain.
- Present PokePlay Studios as the parent brand, PokePlay Live as the stream brand, and PokePlay Store as the shop brand.
- Prioritize national online buyer SEO and communicate nationwide shipping.
- Avoid publishing exact review, sales, follower, or shipping statistics in primary copy so the site does not go stale.
- Link primary shop and stream CTAs to `https://www.whatnot.com/user/pokeplaylive`.
- Promote following PokePlay Live and bookmarking Whatnot shows as the primary way to catch auctions, drops, and giveaways.
- Use logo-inspired colors: charcoal, cream, orange-red, yellow, blue, and green.
- Include an independent seller disclaimer for Pokemon, Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures, and Whatnot.

## V1 Pages
- `/`: homepage for discovery, trust, Whatnot follows, and shop CTAs.
- `/live`: PokePlayLive stream landing page.
- `/shop`: current Whatnot shopping page and future store foundation.
- `/contact`: Whatnot follow and connection hub.

## Future Work
- Add Shopify product data and product schema.
- Add social channels when they exist.
- Add content pages for collector guides and card-buying SEO.
