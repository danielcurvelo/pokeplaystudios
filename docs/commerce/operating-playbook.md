# Commerce Operating Playbook

## Receive a Supplier Shipment

1. Match cartons to the purchase order before opening them.
2. Count each SKU and record shortages, overages, or visible damage.
3. Inspect seals and packaging condition; photograph exceptions.
4. Confirm language, set, barcode, weight, dimensions, and image rights.
5. Calculate landed cost from product cost, freight, duties, and currency fees.
6. Receive accepted units into `Online Store Fulfillment` in Shopify.
7. Publish only listings with complete product data and available stock.

## Prepare a Whatnot Show

1. Choose show inventory and create a transfer to `Whatnot Reserve`.
2. Confirm the reserve location cannot fulfill online orders.
3. Count reserve inventory immediately before the show.
4. After the show, reconcile sold, giveaway, damaged, and unsold units.
5. Return unsold units to `Online Store Fulfillment` with a Shopify transfer.

## Fulfill Shipped Orders

1. Review payment and fraud indicators before picking.
2. Confirm the product language and SKU against the order.
3. Pack to protect seals and product corners; record the packed weight.
4. Add signature confirmation only when the buyer purchased the option.
5. Buy the label, attach tracking, and hand the parcel to the carrier within
   the published one-to-two-business-day processing window.

## Prepare Danceology Pickup

1. Confirm the product is eligible for pickup and the studio is accepting
   handoffs during its published hours.
2. Transfer the order inventory to `Danceology Studio - Draper Pickup`.
3. Do not mark the order ready until it is physically secured at the studio.
4. Send the ready notification with current pickup hours and instructions.
5. At handoff, verify the customer name and order number, then mark picked up.

## Returns and Damage

- Accept unopened returns requested within 14 days, subject to the published
  policy and applicable law.
- Treat opened trading-card products as final sale unless the item was wrong or
  arrived damaged.
- Request photos of damage or an incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery.
- Keep all customer-facing policy text aligned with the final Shopify policies.

## Weekly Control Check

For the first 60 days, count all three inventory locations weekly. Investigate
every variance before adjusting Shopify. Record the reason as receiving error,
Whatnot sale, giveaway, damage, return, transfer, or unknown shrinkage.
